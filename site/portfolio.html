A Lesson I Taught: Uses of Some Tools

On 16th March 2026 I taught a lesson on the uses of everyday tools. Together, the class built up this list on the whiteboard:

  • Screwdriver is used to tighten or loosen a screw.
  • Wheelbarrow is used to carry materials.
  • Scissors is used for cutting.
  • Bottle opener is used for opening a bottle.
  • Knife is used for cutting vegetables.
  • Shovel is used for scooping and mixing sand and water.

Teaching Qualities I Will Focus On

Professional traits I identified in my journal, and why they matter to me.

Enthusiasm

Showing interest and positive energy while teaching.

Respect

Respecting learners, colleagues, members of the school committee and the community.

Creativity

Using different activities and teaching aids to make lessons interesting and easier.

Good Communication

Simple, understandable explanations so learners grasp concepts effectively.

Fairness

Treating all learners fairly, because it builds trust and prevents discrimination and favouritism.

Why a Teaching Portfolio Matters

I shared with my STS partner and mentor that a teaching portfolio template gives a clear structure for documenting teaching experiences, reflecting on classroom practice and monitoring professional growth.

A well-organised portfolio helps student teachers become more confident, reflective, responsible and effective in their careers.
