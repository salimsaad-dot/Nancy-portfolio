About the School

Coach Basic School was established in 2017 with just three children and two caretakers. Its founder, Mr Yaw Owusu Forkuo, is popularly known as “Coach” and is widely recognised at Fadama. He used his nickname to name the school.

The school was fully registered in 2018 and has grown into an outstanding school, with over 100 learners from Basic 2 to Basic 6.

Working with the school: the PTA, parents, the Ghana Education Service, administrators and community members.

Classroom Rules

Rules in the classroom, and why they matter.

Use Polite Words

Words like “please”, “sorry” and “thank you” make for better classroom interaction.

Cooperate with Others

Working together leads to deeper understanding and creates a positive learning environment.

Show Appreciation

Saying thank you when a friend helps you develops good morals.

What I Observed in the Teachers

From my study of teacher professionalism, 28th September 2026.

Friendly & Respectful

The teachers were friendly and approachable, and showed respect to all learners and to their colleagues.

Good Discipline

The teachers maintained good discipline and controlled their classes effectively.

Clear Instructions

The teachers gave clear instructions and explained difficult concepts in simple language.

Challenges

Challenges I saw teachers face in the classroom.

Large Class Sizes

Some teachers struggled to give learners individual attention and to complete all planned activities within the lesson period.

Noise & Attention

Some teachers found it difficult to control noise and keep learners' attention during class periods.
